Our programs

Restorative education, family strengthening, protective care, justice advocacy, and community health — working across Nairobi, Nyeri, and Kisii counties.

Our intervention levels

How we serve children and families

KAI-K operates across five complementary areas, designed to meet children where they are and walk with them toward healing and wholeness.

Restorative Education

We run education and training programs in settings that support healing while children learn. Hall Mead School in Nyeri County provides a caring environment that supports children’s academic, social, spiritual, and physical development.

Nyeri County

Family Strengthening

We work with families to reduce the risks they face and help them grow stronger. Through our Hope Centres in Nairobi, Karundas, and Nyamarambe, we provide parenting support, psychosocial education, livelihood training, and mentorship.

Nairobi, Nyeri & Kisii Counties

Protective Care

When a child has been abused or neglected and cannot safely stay at home, we provide temporary care. Our staff work to meet each child’s needs for health, safety, and emotional healing, with the goal of returning every child to a safe family environment.

All locations

Justice Advocacy

We support children who have experienced abuse to access justice. Our teams include lawyers, psychologists, social workers, and spiritual leads who walk alongside the child and family through the legal process, and advocate for better laws at local, regional, and national levels.

All locations

Community Health

KAI-K owns and operates a clinic in Nyeri County that provides medical services to the local community, in partnership with the Nyeri County Government.

Nyeri County

Where we work

Our programs run in three counties, covering both rural and urban communities. Each Hope Centre connects families with social services, legal support, livelihoods training, and spiritual care.

What we do

Our activities across all locations

Across all our locations, we carry out the following activities to bring healing, justice, and hope to children and families:

  • Therapeutic care and psychosocial support
  • Spiritual discipleship
  • Parenting skills training
  • Psychosocial education for children and caregivers
  • Mentorship skills training
  • Community awareness and justice advocacy
  • Livelihood training and support
  • Capacity building for teachers and church leaders
  • Linkages and partnerships with government and other organisations
  • Partial education support for children in the community

Our team

Multi-disciplinary approach

KAI-K employs social workers, teachers, psychologists, lawyers, and spiritual leads. These staff work together to make sure each child receives care across legal, psychological, educational, and spiritual areas.

Lawyers

Provide legal representation for children in civil and criminal abuse cases.

Psychologists

Help children process trauma and prepare to give testimony in court, using the KAI toolkit.

Social Workers

Support children and families through the justice process and work to ensure the child’s ongoing safety.

Spiritual Leads

Provide faith-based discipleship and support spiritual formation as part of the healing process.
